## Tam açıklama (≤4000)
dizi.jpg is a TV show and movie diary where you track everything you watch episode by episode, discover new titles, and share the journey with a community.

NEVER LOSE YOUR PLACE
• Mark episodes with one tap and watch your season progress fill in
• Organize your library: Watchlist, Watching, Finished, Dropped
• Rate out of 5 stars, write reviews, count your rewatches
• Build favorites and custom lists

CATCH-UP CALENDAR
• Upcoming episodes and releases in a single list
• Grouped by show, so you see exactly how far behind you are
• Get notified when a new episode of a show you follow lands

DEEPER THAN A CHECKLIST
• Watch trailers right inside the app
• Episode heat map: every episode's TMDB score, season by season
• Rating breakdown, so you can see how a title really landed
• Cast, crew, production companies and birthdays

DISCOVER
• Shows and movies of the week, plus recommendations built from your taste
• Browse by genre; search that forgives typos and finds people and studios too
• Where to watch: streaming, rental and purchase options

COMMUNITY
• A feed of comments on the titles you watch, with spoiler protection so you never read about an episode you have not seen
• Post with photos and videos, react with animated emoji
• Follow friends, message them privately, explore profiles
• Comments translate automatically; tap to see the original
• Taste match: see how closely your ratings line up

YOUR STATS
• Total screen time, most-watched shows, your year in review
• Levels and badges as your diary grows

YOUR DATA IS YOURS
• Export everything as a ZIP, import from other apps
• Two-step verification to protect your account
• Hide your watch history or comments any time, per title if you want
• Delete your account completely whenever you want

Available in 45 languages.

Content data provided by TMDB; streaming availability by JustWatch. dizi.jpg is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
